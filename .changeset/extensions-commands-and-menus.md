A shell runs extensions' keys and menu items: `extensions.commands` and `runCommand()` for `chrome.commands`, `extensions.menu()` and `runMenuItem()` for the items an extension adds to its action's menu, and `extensionItems` and `runExtensionItem()` on a page's context menu event.
